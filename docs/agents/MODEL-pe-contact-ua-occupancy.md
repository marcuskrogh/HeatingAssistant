# Model: Contact-gated extra UA and day-gated occupancy in open-loop 2R2C PE

## Problem statement

Identify per-room 2R2C parameters together with two household extras that
today’s estimator mishandles: extra outdoor exchange while a window/door
contact is open, and unmeasured occupancy heat on the air node. The fit
criterion stays open-loop output-error (simulation MSE). Envelope \(C,R\)
are never locked from a night fragment. Hidden wall initial temperature
follows SWD-325.

The primary score is hold-out open-loop indoor-air RMSE (SWD-332). Physical
\(\theta\) recovery is secondary.

## Notation

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| \(T_{\mathrm{a}}, T_{\mathrm{w}}\) | Air and wall states |
| \(T_{\mathrm{out}}\) | Outdoor air (measured) |
| \(c(t)\in\{0,1\}\) | Window/door contact (measured) |
| \(h(t)\) | Hour of day on the room clock |
| \(w_{\mathrm{day}}(t)\) | Day mask: \(1\) if \(h(t)\in[6,23)\), else \(0\) |
| \(UA_{\mathrm{open}}\) | Extra outdoor thermal conductance while \(c=1\) [W/K] |
| \(q_{\mathrm{day}}\) | Daytime occupancy / residual air heat [W] |
| \(\theta_{\mathrm{env}}\) | Existing 2R2C structural parameters (\(C\), \(R_{\mathrm{ext}}\), splits, \(\alpha\), solar scale) |
| \(T_{\mathrm{w}}(t_{0})\) | Wall initial on the estimation window (SWD-325) |
| \(y^{m}\) | Measured indoor air |
| \(J\) | Open-loop simulation MSE of \(T_{\mathrm{a}}\) vs \(y^{m}\) |

## Formulation

**Plant (estimator).** Existing 2R2C. Extra air-node heat, not a third state:

\[
Q_{\mathrm{extra}}(t)
  = UA_{\mathrm{open}}\, c(t)\, \bigl(T_{\mathrm{out}}(t) - T_{\mathrm{a}}(t)\bigr)
  + q_{\mathrm{day}}\, w_{\mathrm{day}}(t).
\]

This replaces the 24-hour-constant `internal_gain`. Occupancy and openings
are different channels: \(q_{\mathrm{day}}\) does not multiply \(c(t)\);
\(UA_{\mathrm{open}}\) does not run while the contact is closed.

**Decision vector** (one room, one time-separated window):

\[
\bigl\{\theta_{\mathrm{env}},\; T_{\mathrm{w}}(t_{0}),\;
UA_{\mathrm{open}},\; q_{\mathrm{day}}\bigr\}.
\]

\(C\) and \(R_{\mathrm{ext}}\) stay free whenever they are identifiable.
Do not night-stage or lock them.

**Contact gating of \(UA_{\mathrm{open}}\).** One scalar per room, not per
opening event. If the estimation window has too few open-contact samples
(\(\sum_k c_k < N_{\min}\), \(N_{\min}\) the existing segment minimum),
fix \(UA_{\mathrm{open}}=0\) and drop it from \(\theta\). Bounds:
\(UA_{\mathrm{open}}\in[0, UA_{\max}]\) with \(UA_{\max}\) a room-scale
cap (order \(50\,\mathrm{W/K}\)).

**Occupancy.** \(q_{\mathrm{day}}\) is a single scalar, applied only while
\(w_{\mathrm{day}}=1\). Identify it jointly with \(\theta_{\mathrm{env}}\)
under the existing MAP prior toward \(0\) (same weight family as today’s
`internal_gain`). Do not use an unregularized outer grid over \(q_{\mathrm{day}}\):
that overfits weak occupancy. Do not introduce an occupancy state trajectory
as PE decisions (too many degrees of freedom for open-loop OE on a day of
data).

**Open-contact samples.** Once \(UA_{\mathrm{open}}\) is in the model,
include those samples in \(J\). The SWD-322 exclude-open-window mask is the
wrong residual treatment for this estimator.

**Linearisation in the fit.** During OE, it is admissible to replace
\(T_{\mathrm{a}}\) in the UA term by the measurement \(y^{m}\) so the extra
conductance acts as a known input. The free-run predictor (validation and
MPC) uses simulated \(T_{\mathrm{a}}\). Occupancy uses the clock mask on
both fit and prediction; it never uses plant occupancy watts.

**Objective.** Unchanged open-loop simulation MSE of indoor air, including
open-contact steps, plus the existing light MAP regularisation. Not
Kalman/PED one-step likelihood.

**Hidden wall.** \(T_{\mathrm{w}}(t_{0})\) per SWD-325 (leading-window box).
\(T_{\mathrm{w}}(t)\) for \(t>t_{0}\) is generated by the open-loop 2R2C
simulation.

## Assumptions

- Contacts are observed; occupancy is not. Occupancy can only be a
  clock-parameterized disturbance that transfers to the next day.
- Household night is empty enough that a day mask is the right one-parameter
  occupancy model. Bursts inside the day are absorbed into \(q_{\mathrm{day}}\)
  and residual envelope error; they are not a second identified schedule.
- 2R2C structure is unchanged. Extras are additive air-node heat.
- Open-loop OE remains the right \(J\) for this destination (SWD-332).

## Algorithmic choices

- **Do** identify \(UA_{\mathrm{open}}\) (do not freeze \(15\,\mathrm{W/K}\)).
- **Do** replace 24 h `internal_gain` with day-gated \(q_{\mathrm{day}}\)
  under the same prior toward \(0\).
- **Do not** lock \(C,R\) on night fragments (`occupancy_tv`).
- **Do not** put a slowly varying occupancy *state* into the OE decision
  vector, and do not switch \(J\) to CD-EKF PED to recover Coffman–Barooah
  SPDI. That family lost on hold-out free-run in SWD-332.
- **Fallback** if a later bake-off shows closed-window regression vs
  `today_combined`: keep identified \(UA_{\mathrm{open}}\) and revert
  occupancy to 24 h `internal_gain`. Identified UA alone already beats
  assumed-UA on the SWD-329/332 grid without closed-window regression.

## Numerical considerations

- \(N_{\min}\) reuses the estimator’s existing minimum segment length.
- \(UA_{\max}\approx 50\,\mathrm{W/K}\) is a cap, not a physical law.
- Day mask \([6,23)\) matches the SWD-329 household night; define may
  rename it to a product “night empty” clock but must not lock \(C,R\).
- Outer discrete grids over \(UA_{\mathrm{open}}\) or \(q_{\mathrm{day}}\)
  are investigation tools only. Production PE should put both in the joint
  L-BFGS-B vector with analytic sensitivities.

## Numerical check (harness-only)

Open-loop fits on the SWD-329/332 9-scenario hold-out, `maxiter=25`, same
plant. Assumed-UA \(15\,\mathrm{W/K}\) reproduces the SWD-332 mean
**0.83 °C**.

| Procedure | Mean val RMSE | Closed-window (none / weak / strong occ) |
|-----------|---------------|------------------------------------------|
| Assumed UA \(15\,\mathrm{W/K}\) + 24 h \(q_{\mathrm{int}}\) | 0.83 °C | 0.45 / 0.46 / 0.99 °C |
| Identified UA + 24 h \(q_{\mathrm{int}}\) | **0.64 °C** | 0.45 / 0.46 / 0.99 °C (no regression) |
| Identified UA + unregularized day-\(q\) grid | 0.47 °C | 0.45 / **0.52** / 0.48 °C (weak-occ regression) |

Identifying UA is the reliable gain. Day-gating occupancy is the remaining
gain, but only if \(q_{\mathrm{day}}\) is jointly regularized toward \(0\)
(the unregularized grid overstated weak occupancy). Worst remaining assumed-UA
cell (`occ_weak` + weak window, 1.81 °C) fell to 0.55 °C with identified UA
alone.
