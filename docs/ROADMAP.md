# Roadmap: Parameter estimation effectiveness and guidance

## Destination
Reliably identify a thermal model with high predictive power for real rooms. On synthetic data, recover true parameters as well as possible. Working setup: one 2R2C room for simulation and 2R2C for estimation (independent choices; either can change later).

## Notes
- Sensors: standard indoor climate (e.g. IKEA TIMMERFLOTE); no extra wall-temperature sensors.
- Parameter estimation stays per-room. Open doors/windows and occupancy are extra heat the estimator does not see, not a multi-room plant.
- Synthesise now; real home recordings come later as a separate check.
- 2R2C is the working hypothesis, not a lock. Reevaluate structure if recovery or prediction fails.
- SWD-324: production PE jointly fits wall initial temperature \(T_{w,0}\) with \(R,C\); leading-window reconstruction exists but is unused by PE.
- SWD-325: \(T_{w}(t_{0})\) is a PE decision per time-separated window, boxed by a 24 h indoor/outdoor range \(\pm 25\%\) width; \(T_{w}(t)\) is generated by open-loop simulation or Kalman filter (fork).
- SWD-326: offline combined vs separated/staged bake-off; no product winner. Report `docs/agents/REPORT-pe-dataset-separation.md`.
- SWD-328: occupancy is extra air-node heat; open window/door is extra outdoor exchange, not the same term. True \(C,R\) recovery needs clean regimes or those extras in the estimator. Artifact `docs/agents/RESEARCH-pe-robustness-household.md`.

## Not yet specified
- Exact disturbance catalogue for the synthetic (window/door, occupancy, other extras).
- Extra estimated terms or a different RC structure if 2R2C plant + 2R2C estimator fails.

## Out of scope
- A multi-room plant (PE remains per-room).
- Additional sensors beyond standard indoor temperature/humidity.
- MPC / controller behaviour changes.
